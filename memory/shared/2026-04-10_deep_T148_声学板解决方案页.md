# Acoustic Panel & Soundproof Board Cutting Solution | Oscillating Knife Cutter

## Cut Acoustic Panels Cleanly Without Burn Marks or Heavy Dust
Manufacturers of acoustic panels, soundproof boards, and sound-absorbing materials often face the same production problems: laser cutting can leave burnt edges and heat-affected zones, while saw cutting creates excessive dust, rough edges, and unstable dimensional accuracy. These issues increase rework, slow down production, and make it harder to deliver clean, high-quality panels for architectural, industrial, and interior applications.

## Why Choose an Oscillating Knife Cutting Solution?
HongTaiYuan CNC oscillating knife cutting machines provide a cold-cutting process designed for soft, semi-rigid, and composite acoustic materials.

### Key Benefits
- **High precision:** up to **±0.1 mm** cutting accuracy for consistent panel size and shape
- **No thermal deformation:** no burnt edges, no melting, and no heat damage compared with laser cutting
- **Low dust process:** cleaner workshop environment than traditional saw cutting
- **Better edge quality:** smooth and neat edges, reducing secondary finishing work
- **Eco-friendly production:** lower material waste, cleaner cutting, and improved operator safety
- **Flexible cutting capability:** suitable for custom shapes, holes, grooves, and sample-to-mass production workflows
