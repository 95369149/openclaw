# 高质量图片提示词模板 v2.0
> 基于 Google 官方 Nano Banana 2 示例 + 碰撞优化

## 核心原则
1. **提示词长度 150-300 词**（官方示例平均水平）
2. **必须包含**：摄影术语 + 情绪描述 + 具体色彩 + 材质细节
3. **用 16:9 格式**（后期可裁切为 9:16）

## 结构公式（7要素）
```
[主体+细节描述 50词] + [场景环境 30词] + [光线方向+质感 20词] +
[镜头类型+参数 15词] + [风格+参考 15词] + [色调+色彩 15词] + [氛围+情绪 15词]
```

## 6图故事法（关键帧生成）
```
Create a cinematic 6-part visual story about [主题].
The story arc: [开头] → [发展] → [高潮] → [结尾].
Keep visual style consistent across all 6 images.
Each image should be a separate output in 16:9 format.
Style: [风格描述].
```

## 赛道模板

### 1. 视觉奇观
```
This aerial shot captures a dramatic, misty landscape of [场景], characterized by
rolling verdant hills and a winding river. The photography style leans towards a moody
and atmospheric aesthetic, emphasizing the grandeur and isolation of nature. The camera
angle is high, looking down into the valley, providing a sweeping panoramic view that
highlights the immense scale of the surroundings. The dominant colors are various shades
of deep green, ranging from lush emerald in the foreground to darker, more muted tones
on the distant mountains. The sky is heavily laden with clouds and mist that cling to
the mountain peaks, creating a soft, diffused lighting style that minimizes harsh shadows
and enhances the overall ethereal feel. Shot on Hasselblad X2D, 45mm lens, f/8,
award-winning landscape photography. 16:9 format.
```

### 2. 知识科普
```
A clean, modern infographic-style illustration explaining [知识点]. The composition
features a central diagram with labeled components, surrounded by supporting visual
elements. The background is a soft gradient from white to light grey, with subtle grid
lines suggesting precision and accuracy. Typography is clean sans-serif in dark charcoal.
Key elements are highlighted in [品牌色: electric blue / coral red]. Soft, even studio
lighting with no harsh shadows. The overall aesthetic is premium educational content,
similar to Kurzgesagt or Vox explainer style. Minimal, elegant, information-dense.
4K resolution, 16:9 format.
```

### 3. 艺术创意
```
Cinematic still, evoking a vibrant, dreamlike quality often found in highly stylized
visual art. The camera is positioned [角度], emphasizing [效果]. The color palette is
exceptionally bold and high-contrast, dominated by [主色] and [辅色], with a [点缀色]
accent. The subject is [主体详细描述 50词，包含服装、表情、姿态、材质]. The background
is [背景描述]. The ambiance is one of [情绪], imbued with an almost surreal, pop-art
energy. Soft diffused lighting with subtle rim light. Shot in the style of [参考艺术家/风格].
8K resolution, extremely detailed, 16:9 format.
```

### 4. 故事叙事
```
An intimate, naturalistic cinematic close-up reveals [主体详细描述]. Delicate [光线描述]
filters through [环境元素], casting gentle shadows. The background is a soft blur of
[背景色彩], emphasizing [情感主题]. The subject's expression conveys [情绪: contemplation/
determination/quiet joy]. Shallow depth of field, shot on 35mm film with visible grain,
warm color grading reminiscent of [参考电影/导演]. The overall mood is [氛围描述].
Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

### 5. 制造/科技
```
A massive [设备名称] in full operation inside a state-of-the-art factory floor.
[具体动作描述: precision cutting through 10mm steel plate / robotic arm welding at
2000°C]. Dramatic side lighting streams through floor-to-ceiling industrial windows,
creating volumetric light beams through airborne particles and [特效: sparks / steam /
metal dust]. The machine's surface shows [材质细节: brushed aluminum / matte black
carbon fiber / polished chrome]. Wide angle shot from slightly below eye level,
emphasizing the machine's imposing presence. Industrial documentary photography style,
shot on RED V-Raptor, 24mm lens. Color palette: warm orange sparks contrasting with
cool blue-grey steel. 4K ultra sharp, 16:9 format.
```
