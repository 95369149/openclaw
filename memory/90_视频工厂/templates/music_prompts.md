# 配乐提示词模板

## 结构公式
```
[情绪] + [风格] + [节奏/BPM] + [乐器] + [时长] + [用途]
```

## 赛道配乐模板

### 1. 视觉奇观
```
Epic cinematic orchestral music, slowly building from quiet strings
to full orchestra crescendo, 80-100 BPM, majestic and awe-inspiring,
with deep bass drums and soaring violins, 30 seconds,
background music for nature documentary short video
```

### 2. 知识科普
```
Light upbeat electronic music, clean and modern feel,
steady 110 BPM rhythm, soft synth pads with gentle piano melody,
curious and engaging mood, 30 seconds,
background music for educational explainer video
```

### 3. 艺术创意
```
Ambient ethereal music, dreamy floating atmosphere,
slow 70 BPM, reverb-heavy piano with atmospheric synth textures,
mysterious and beautiful, 30 seconds,
background music for artistic visual content
```

### 4. 故事叙事
```
Emotional cinematic piano piece, intimate and touching,
slow 60-70 BPM, solo piano with subtle string accompaniment,
builds from quiet reflection to hopeful resolution, 30 seconds,
background music for storytelling short film
```

### 5. 制造/科技
```
Industrial electronic music with mechanical rhythmic elements,
powerful and precise, 120 BPM, deep bass synth with metallic percussion,
futuristic and confident, 30 seconds,
background music for factory/technology showcase video
```

## 配乐匹配原则
- 视频节奏快 → 配乐BPM高（120+）
- 视频节奏慢 → 配乐BPM低（60-80）
- 震撼场景 → 管弦乐/史诗感
- 温馨场景 → 钢琴/吉他
- 科技场景 → 电子/合成器
- 配乐音量控制在视频音量的30%左右
- 有旁白时配乐更轻，纯画面时配乐可以更重
